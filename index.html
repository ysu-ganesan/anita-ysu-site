Enterprise

An AI department inside your building.

Factories, plants and engineering teams run on repetitive knowledge work, and their data can't go to the cloud. So we come to them: a team of agents built around how the company already works.

  1. On your premisesBuilt to run on your own machines, so drawings, prices and people's names stay in the building.
  2. Agents draft, people signEvery quote, report and approval is drafted by an agent and signed by a person. AI never signs, and every step is logged.
  3. One workflow firstWe agree the success numbers on day one and measure them on day 30. If it doesn't clear the bar, you keep the report.
  4. The role remembersDesigned to learn the job beside your people, so when someone moves on, their know-how stays.
